ASSESSMENT
SCOPE: Independent noncommercial sports-analytics portfolio. Logos identify the actual subjects of analysis. No ads, logo merchandise, paid logo service, claim of partnership or use as Fourth Down branding.

TRADEMARK: A nominative/referential-use argument is plausible when the team is the subject, the mark is subordinate to the analysis and no endorsement is suggested. Names alone could identify teams, so use of graphic marks is not automatically protected.

PURPOSE: The purpose is contextual team identification within statistical analysis and model explanation. Portfolio self-promotion is acknowledged; noncommercial or educational presentation is not an automatic exemption. The artwork is not itself criticized and identification overlaps its original purpose.

NATURE: Many emblems are creative, published copyrighted artwork; this weighs against fair use where copyright subsists. Commons simple-shape/text and publication-without-notice labels are recorded separately as claims, not assumed worldwide rights.

AMOUNT: A whole emblem is needed for accurate visual recognition, but only one small PNG per team is bundled, 250 pixels wide, at most 100000 pixels total, displayed at 156 pixels or less. Complete reproduction still weighs in the assessment; no vector originals are shipped.

MARKET: The small contextual images are not substitutes for merchandise or production-quality art. A licensing market may nevertheless exist; no claim of zero market effect or guaranteed fair use is made.

CONCLUSION: A reasonable limited informational-use position is recorded for this exact presentation, with residual copyright, trademark and source-status uncertainty. Wikipedia fair-use rationales do not transfer. No owner authorization or legal clearance is claimed.
