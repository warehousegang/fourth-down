FOURTH DOWN — CURRENT SCHEDULE DATA ATTRIBUTION

Current-season schedules: SportsDataverse / NFL Next Gen Stats.
https://github.com/sportsdataverse/sportsdataverse-data/releases/tag/nfl_ngs_schedules
Source capture/processing: https://github.com/sportsdataverse/nfl-ngs-data
Upstream: public NFL Next Gen Stats API.
The release documents direct no-key download of separate per-season assets.
Repository license: https://github.com/sportsdataverse/sportsdataverse-data/blob/main/LICENSE
Checked 2026-10-24. Complete repository license follows.

Fourth Down selects regular-season schedule/results fields, normalizes team
abbreviations and derives frozen-model features and probabilities. No tracking,
player-stat, news, injury or weather inputs are used. No NFL/ESPN logo assets
are redistributed. The forecast is an independent derived research output;
neither the provider nor the NFL endorses it. Upstream rights remain subject
to separate public-release review. This local build does not authorize publication.

nflverse / nflverse-pbp, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.
Source: https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-pbp/releases
License: https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-pbp/blob/master/LICENSE.md

Fourth Down reads only explicitly selected current-season, per-game published files.
Derived output: normalized game status, verified final score, and prospective V1
prediction scores. Original raw bytes and observation timestamps are retained.
This result adapter does not change forecast inputs, model coefficients, or
archived probabilities. nflverse and the NFL do not endorse Fourth Down.
