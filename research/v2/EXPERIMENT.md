# V2 efficiency challenger — fixed experiment 1

Declared before fitting on 2026-10-25. Research only; no production replacement, public deployment, automatic promotion or changes to the existing model registry. The prior feasibility recommendation is the seven-feature core below; quarterback, injury, weather and news remain non-predictive context. The existing `docs/CHALLENGER_PROTOCOL.md` and locked, partially exposed `docs/HOLDOUT_PROTOCOL.md` remain authoritative for their original experiments.

## Data and time boundary

Use the preserved 2010–2023 REG schedule, SHA256 `20f573837dac7d9ea41dade07b165de6164b3370d60e49d5242e5255cabb7d01`, and exactly the fourteen season-specific nflverse PBP parquet assets for 2010–2023. Never request combined seasons or 2024–2025, including as previous-year inputs. Pin raw checksums, retrieval/update timestamps and derived team-game totals. 2010 is warmup; labeled training starts 2011. Ties update history but are excluded from binary labels. The canceled 2022 BUF–CIN game is not a completed observation. Every unavailable row is reported; comparisons use identical eligible rows and no outcome-dependent selection.

Cutoff: 00:00 America/New_York on target game date; only strictly earlier dates, same-date batches read before any updates. Historical files are corrected extracts, not publication-vintage evidence. This is an event-time retrospective experiment, not demonstrated prospective availability. Missing preceding scores, missing/invalid required efficiency totals, or missing previous-season score history disable the affected row; do not silently impute a missing game as Week 1. An unavailable opponent's earlier raw efficiency propagates into subsequent adjusted state. Team aliases STL→LA, SD→LAC, OAK→LV, LAR→LA, AZ→ARI preserve franchise continuity. State resets each season.

## Exactly seven predictors (home minus away)

1–4 retain `ml/features.py` exactly: `(wins+2)/(games+4)`, cumulative scoring margin divided by `games+4`, last-five margin sum divided by `min(games,5)+2`, and rest capped at 14 days (7 before first game). Ties count half a win.

5. Previous-season scoring margin: sum of completed REG margins divided by `previous_games+4`; missing previous-season history disables the row. No prior efficiency or protected 2025 strength is substituted.

6–7. Opponent-adjusted offensive yards/play and defensive yards allowed/play. A counted scrimmage play has `play_type` pass or run, `qb_kneel=0`, `qb_spike=0`, `two_point_attempt=0`; sacks/scrambles remain included, no-play/penalty-only plays and returns excluded. Require finite yards and valid identities/flags for every such play; invalid totals disable that game for future state. These are deliberately defined research ratios, not a claim of matching official box-score YPP. Each team-game needs positive offensive and defensive play counts. Yards allowed derive from the opponent's same counted plays.

Let the fixed, non-estimated neutral reference be 5.5 yards/play and shrinkage four neutral games. Before completed game g, opponent raw offense rating is `sum(own_YPP−5.5)/(n+4)` and defense rating is `sum(allowed_YPP−5.5)/(n+4)` using only its earlier games. Store g's adjusted offense as `own_YPP−5.5−opponent_pregame_defense` and defense as `allowed_YPP−5.5−opponent_pregame_offense`. At target cutoff, each team's adjusted rating is the sum of these stored game values divided by `n+4`. Subtract away from home. Positive defensive values mean allowing more yards. Ratings are equal-game-weighted, not pooled by plays. Week 1 current ratings are zero; previous-season margin remains real. Never recompute an earlier adjustment with the opponent's later or final-season rating.

## One estimator and chronological evaluation

No search/ablations/feature selection in this version. StandardScaler and L2 logistic regression: C=1, lbfgs, max_iter=2000, random_state=42, no class weighting. For each diagnostic season 2018–2023: train 2011 through season−2; fit scaler/base model only there; fit Platt logistic calibration on season−1 raw log odds, C=1,000,000 with the same solver/seed/iteration limit; evaluate next season. Calibrated predictions are primary, raw predictions also reported. Apply the identical calibration procedure to the contemporaneously refitted four-feature baseline, isolating feature contribution. Also report raw four-feature, training home-win frequency and .5. Final research candidate is the 2023 fold (base 2011–2021, calibration 2022); no refit on diagnostics.

Report per-season/pooled accuracy at .5, Brier (primary), log loss, ten fixed equal-width calibration bins including empty bins, ECE, eligible/excluded counts and reasons. Probabilities condition on non-tied outcomes. Separately report frozen V1 on 2023 only; it has a longer fit window and selection exposure, so is not the controlled comparator. All diagnostic seasons are reused research data, not pristine tests.

Uncertainty: 2,000 paired replicates, numpy default_rng(42), sorted season/week/game IDs, sample whole `(season,week)` blocks with replacement within each season, retaining its original block count. Compute game-weighted metrics from concatenated draws, using identical draws for models and paired differences. Report percentile 95% intervals for metrics and V2-minus-baseline differences; reset RNG for each per-season/pooled report. Negative Brier/log-loss differences favor V2; no reliable improvement claim when interval includes zero. Cross-week team dependence, reuse and corrected vintages limit inference.

## Fixed decision and isolation

Carry forward research thresholds: pooled Brier improvement ≥.002 over calibrated four-feature comparator with paired upper 95% bound <0; no season Brier regression >.01; pooled ECE≤.05 and every season≤.10. Report every result regardless of sign, never tune this configuration afterward. Promotion additionally requires point-in-time operational data, at least one prospective shadow season and explicit owner approval. Historical success alone cannot approve production.

Store code under `ml/v2_*`, models/evidence under `research/v2/`, raw downloads under ignored `data/research-v2/`, shadow records under a separate ignored research directory. No web/public, operational registry, current forecasts, model evidence or workflow writes. Shadow infrastructure is local-only and disabled for 2026 because 2025 inputs are protected. Missing required inputs also disable inference. Historical replay never becomes a prospective receipt. Shadow comparison preserves V1/V2 probabilities and timestamps and produces separate immutable reconciliation versions; no automatic promotion.
