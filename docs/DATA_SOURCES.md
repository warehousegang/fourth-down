Use 2010–2023 regular-season schedule rows. Required fields: game_id, season, week, game_type, gameday, gametime, home_team, away_team, home_score, away_score; stadium/location optional. No market odds, postgame statistics, injuries or news enter predictors. Scheduled kickoff and stadium are display metadata. Score-derived fields use only earlier game dates. Season and team identifiers index records. Ties update history at half a win, but are excluded as binary evaluation targets. Missing outcomes never update history.

That loader is not used by the current-forecast adapter. No automatic refresh. A later source correction can change reproduction; use the captured snapshot for exact reruns.

## Current-season source (additive, 2026-10-24)

The authorized vertical slice uses the public, per-season [SportsDataverse NFL Next Gen Stats schedule release](https://github.com/sportsdataverse/sportsdataverse-data/releases/tag/nfl_ngs_schedules). Its [producer documentation](https://github.com/sportsdataverse/nfl-ngs-data/blob/main/README.md) describes NFL Next Gen Stats schedule acquisition. [Repository MIT permission](https://github.com/sportsdataverse/sportsdataverse-data/blob/main/LICENSE) was retrieved and preserved verbatim in web/public/CURRENT_DATA_NOTICE.txt, with copyright 2023 SportsDataverse, provenance and derived-work labeling. This permits the local implementation on the repository's stated terms; it is not a claim that all upstream NFL redistribution rights are independently settled. Public release remains gated.

The adapter reads GitHub release metadata, selects exactly `ngs_schedule_<current-season>.csv`, validates its documented SHA256 digest, then checks every CSV row's season before normalizing results. It does not retrieve any other season file or the combined nflverse schedules. The initial 2026 asset has SHA256 `1922b31983e74a7c53c661084c7d68e70bd9ea2ffd63ff410aaa754dc5023d9c`, published 2026-10-23T11:47:43Z, containing 49 PRE and 272 REG rows. Only REG rows enter state; 94 were final at capture. Captured bytes and retrieval bounds are under ignored data/current/2026/. Public forecasts contain no target outcomes or raw schedule rows.

Use source game_key as stable identity and retain the original game_id separately. Normalize AZ → ARI and LAR → LA. game_time must be timezone-aware UTC; derive the model's calendar date in America/New_York. A date without a confirmed time does not yield a probability. Explicit FINAL / FINAL OVERTIME, validated=true and both scores are required to update history; kickoff alone never proves completion. Required coverage is 272 games, 32 teams × 17 games, distinct stable IDs and one game per team per nominal week. A coverage change fails closed pending review rather than silently changing eligibility.

Only final observations strictly before each cutoff are eligible. The source does not supply a trusted completion timestamp, so immutable retrieval observations provide conservative upper bounds. Corrected records cannot inherit earlier proof. The source update and retrieval timestamps differ and are both shown. Warnings start after 24 hours of source age; forecasts expire at the earlier of source update + 48 hours or generation + 24 hours. Browser time and actual kickoff provide further suppression. These are operational freshness guards, not fitted model thresholds. They do not guarantee that the provider updated every row promptly.

## Product intelligence expansion
The product directive requires all 12 domains to have explicit source, license, vintage, transformation, missingness and contribution records. INTELLIGENCE_DOMAINS.md is that domain register. A candidate source is not an ingested or trained feature. Expanded schedule-derived challengers may use only the already verified 2010–2023 snapshot; current 2026 prior-year strength cannot consume the locked 2025 season. Player, efficiency, injury, weather and news contexts require permitted, timestamped observations; any missing vintage or permission remains unavailable rather than inferred from a later page. Result reconciliation uses the same current-season FINAL/validation/observation checks as current acquisition and never fabricates pregame forecasts for earlier completed games.
