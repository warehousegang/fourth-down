See PROJECT.md for the authoritative record and G0–G4 evidence links.

## Purpose and success
Produce real upcoming-game probabilities with inspectable inputs and provenance; retain immutable forecasts; observe and score every eligible completed prediction; investigate surprising outcomes with evidence; propose and validate challengers under human promotion control. Success is an auditable Forecast → Observe → Evaluate → Investigate → Propose → Train challenger → Validate → Approve → Promote loop. Unsupported analytics, silently changed models and invented data fail acceptance regardless of visual polish.

## Incumbent prediction-time contract
The incumbent remains schedule-v1-741cf41752 with its exact Record.values formulas, absorbed training scaler, raw coefficients, feature order, tie history, season reset, rest cap and p ≥ 0.5 classification rule. Its cutoff is min(actual decision time, target date at 00:00 America/New_York). Eligible final observations must precede this cutoff and come from strictly earlier game dates. First observations after cutoff cannot prove earlier availability; do not invent end timestamps. Prior-season strength, QB/injuries, weather/news and efficiency are not incumbent inputs. Challenger availability cannot be used to imply they affect incumbent forecasts.

Current acquisition physically isolates the exact non-protected season (initially 2026). Never use the combined nflverse asset or consume 2024–2025 outcomes. The legacy aggregate exposure remains disclosed as partially exposed. Expansion does not grant access to a protected prior season merely because it would improve a current feature.

## Boundaries
Local implementation, tests, prospective current-season evaluation and separately versioned challenger experiments on authorized data are permitted. Earlier increment's blanket no-fit/no-evaluation wording is superseded **only for separate challengers and current-season lifecycle evaluation**; incumbent preservation and holdout restrictions remain. No public deployment, scheduled public workflow activation, paid API/infrastructure, account changes, personal-site edits or model promotion without explicit approval. No private mental-state inference. No persistent backend unless a demonstrated requirement justifies one.
